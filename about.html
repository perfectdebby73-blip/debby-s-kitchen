Our Culinary Story

Passionate About Nigerian Home Cooking

Welcome to Debby's Kitchen, where delicious food meets care, quality, and great taste. Our goal is to provide enjoyable meals and a satisfying food experience for every customer.

Debby's Kitchen was born out of a profound love for traditional Nigerian cooking. From steaming pots of party jollof rice to nourishing native soups like Egusi and Efo Riro, we honor the recipes and methods that make Nigerian cuisine so widely celebrated.

Whether serving students on campus, working professionals on their lunch break, or families celebrating at home, we cook each meal with individual care, prompt attention, and unmatched culinary passion.
